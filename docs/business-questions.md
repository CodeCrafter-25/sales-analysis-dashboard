# Business Questions

The Sales Analysis Dashboard is designed to answer the following business questions:

1. How did sales and modeled profit change between 2015 and 2018?

2. Which product categories generated the highest sales and modeled profit?

3. Which products contributed the most to total modeled profit?

4. Which customers generated the highest sales?

5. How does modeled ROI differ across product categories?

6. Which periods showed the strongest and weakest sales performance?

## Important Note

The profitability metrics in this project are based on modeled category-level margins:

- Office Supplies: 20%
- Furniture: 30%
- Technology: 40%

Therefore, profit and ROI findings represent a portfolio analysis scenario rather than actual company accounting results.
