# Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The Sales Analysis Dashboard covers the period from 2015 to 2018.

Key portfolio metrics:

- Total Sales: approximately $2.26M
- Total Cost: approximately $1.57M
- Modeled Profit: approximately $0.69M
- Overall ROI: 43.97%

The analysis shows a generally positive long-term sales and profitability trend, strong seasonal performance in Q4, and Technology as the strongest category by modeled profitability.


## 1. Sales and Profit Trend

### Key Results

- Highest-sales quarter: Q4 2018 — approximately $277,770
- Lowest-sales quarter: Q1 2016 — approximately $62,358
- Highest modeled-profit quarter: Q4 2018 — approximately $85,418
- Lowest modeled-profit quarter: Q1 2016 — approximately $18,453

Annual sales:

- 2015: approximately $479.6K
- 2016: approximately $459.3K
- 2017: approximately $599.5K
- 2018: approximately $721.4K

Annual modeled profit:

- 2015: approximately $146.3K
- 2016: approximately $140.7K
- 2017: approximately $183.8K
- 2018: approximately $219.4K

### Insight

Sales declined slightly in 2016 but then increased strongly in 2017 and 2018.

Compared with 2015, sales in 2018 were approximately 50.4% higher, while modeled profit increased by approximately 49.9%.

The quarterly pattern also indicates seasonality: Q4 is consistently one of the strongest periods, while Q1 tends to show weaker performance.


## 2. Category Performance

Modeled profit by category:

- Technology: $330,824 — 47.93% of total modeled profit
- Furniture: $218,428 — 31.65%
- Office Supplies: $140,938 — 20.42%

Based on the modeled category margins, estimated category sales are approximately:

- Technology: $827K
- Furniture: $728K
- Office Supplies: $705K

Modeled ROI by category:

- Technology: approximately 66.67%
- Furniture: approximately 42.86%
- Office Supplies: 25.00%

### Insight

Technology is the strongest category across modeled profitability metrics.

It generates the highest estimated sales, the highest modeled profit, and the highest modeled ROI. Technology alone contributes almost 48% of total modeled profit.

Office Supplies is the weakest category by modeled profit and ROI.


## 3. Top Products

Top products by modeled profit:

1. Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier — $24,640
2. Cisco TelePresence System EX90 — $9,055
3. Hewlett Packard LaserJet 3310 Copier — $7,536

The Canon imageCLASS product generates more than 2.7 times the modeled profit of the second-ranked product.

The combined modeled profit of the Top-10 products is approximately $83,861, representing about 12.15% of total modeled profit.

Canon represents approximately 29.38% of the profit generated by the Top-10 products, but only about 3.57% of total modeled profit across the full dataset.

### Insight

Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is a clear profitability outlier among the leading products.

However, the Top-10 products together account for only about 12% of total modeled profit, suggesting that profitability is distributed across a relatively broad product portfolio rather than being concentrated only in a few products.


## 4. Top Customers

Top customers by sales:

1. Sean Miller — $25,043
2. Tamara Chand — $19,052
3. Raymond Buch — $15,117

The Top-10 customers generated approximately $153,810 in sales.

This represents approximately 6.81% of total project sales.

Sean Miller, the largest individual customer, represents approximately 1.11% of total sales.

### Insight

Sales are not heavily dependent on a small number of customers.

Even the Top-10 customers account for less than 7% of total sales, indicating a relatively diversified customer base and limited concentration risk.


## Key Findings

1. Sales and modeled profit show strong long-term growth, despite a temporary decline in 2016.

2. Q4 consistently performs strongly, suggesting a clear seasonal effect.

3. Technology is the main modeled profitability driver, contributing almost half of total modeled profit.

4. Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the strongest individual product by modeled profit.

5. Profit is distributed across a broad product portfolio, with the Top-10 products contributing only about 12% of total modeled profit.

6. Customer concentration is relatively low, with the Top-10 customers generating less than 7% of total sales.


## Methodology Note

Cost, Profit, Margin, and ROI are modeled metrics created for this portfolio project.

The profitability model uses fixed category-level margins:

- Office Supplies: 20%
- Furniture: 30%
- Technology: 40%

Therefore, profitability-related insights represent an analytical scenario and should not be interpreted as actual company accounting results.
